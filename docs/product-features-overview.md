# Streamora — Product Features Overview

**Audience:** Business stakeholders, partners, creators, and non-technical team members  
**Purpose:** Explain what Streamora does, why each feature matters, and what makes the platform trustworthy and differentiated.

---

## What is Streamora?

Streamora is a **video publishing platform** where creators upload videos, the platform prepares them for smooth playback on any device, administrators review content before it goes public, and viewers discover and watch videos through a modern web experience.

Think of it as: **YouTube-style publishing + editorial control + enterprise-grade reliability** — built for organizations that care about **quality, trust, and who appears on their platform**.

---

## Who is it for?

| Role | Who they are | What they do on Streamora |
|------|----------------|---------------------------|
| **Viewer (Guest)** | Anyone visiting the site | Browse, search, watch, and share videos |
| **Creator** | People who upload content | Upload videos, add titles/descriptions, choose thumbnails, submit for review |
| **Admin / Moderator** | Platform operators | Approve or reject videos, publish approved content, manage takedowns |

---

## Our three core promises

1. **Trust first** — Nothing goes public until the platform approves it. New creators’ identities stay hidden until verified.
2. **Works on mobile** — Uploading and watching are designed for phones, not just desktop.
3. **Professional playback** — Videos are automatically converted for fast, reliable streaming worldwide.

---

## Feature guide (by journey)

---

### 1. Secure sign-in and roles

**What it is**  
Users log in with a secure account. The system knows whether someone is a viewer, creator, admin, or moderator, and shows the right tools to each person.

**Why it matters**  
- Only authorized people can upload or approve content  
- Admins cannot accidentally use creator tools (and vice versa)  
- Protects the platform from anonymous abuse  

**What makes it stand out**  
Clear separation between **“people who make content”** and **“people who govern the platform”** — essential for branded or curated video services, not open anonymous upload sites.

---

### 2. Resumable video upload (upload-first flow)

**What it is**  
Creators upload video files directly to secure cloud storage. If the connection drops, upload can resume instead of starting over. After upload, they add title, description, tags, and other details.

**Why it matters**  
- Large videos (hundreds of MB to GB) are practical on home Wi‑Fi and mobile networks  
- Failed uploads don’t waste the creator’s time  
- Upload completes quickly from the creator’s perspective — heavy processing happens in the background  

**What makes it stand out**  
**Upload first, polish later.** Creators aren’t blocked by forms before the file is safely stored. This matches how people actually work: “get the file in, then fix the details.”

---

### 3. Automatic video processing (the “magic behind the scenes”)

**What it is**  
Once a video is uploaded, the platform automatically:

- Reads video length and quality  
- Creates **6 preview images (thumbnails)** spread across the video  
- Converts the video into **streaming format (HLS)** at two quality levels (standard and HD)  
- Stores everything ready for playback  

Status progression: **Uploaded → Processing → Ready**

**Why it matters**  
| Without processing | With processing |
|--------------------|-----------------|
| Huge files, slow to play | Small chunks, fast start |
| Poor experience on mobile | Adapts to slow or fast internet |
| No preview images | Rich cards and social previews |
| Manual work for IT | Fully automatic |

**What makes it stand out**  
Creators upload **once**. The platform handles the technical work that Netflix and YouTube do internally — so viewers get a smooth experience without creators needing video engineering skills.

---

### 4. Multi-language content (English, Sinhala, Tamil)

**What it is**  
Creators can enter title, description, and tagline in **English, Sinhala, and Tamil**. Viewers see content in their chosen language when available.

**Why it matters**  
- Serves local and regional audiences  
- One video can speak to multiple language communities  
- Important for Sri Lankan and multilingual markets  

**What makes it stand out**  
Built-in from the start — not an afterthought. Positions Streamora for **regional media, education, and community platforms**.

---

### 5. Draft editor and metadata

**What it is**  
A full editing workspace where creators set:

- Title, tagline, description  
- Channels and tags (categories)  
- Visibility (public, unlisted, private)  
- Optional publish schedule  
- Thumbnails and subtitles  

**Why it matters**  
- Professional presentation increases views and trust  
- Categories help viewers find related content  
- Creators can save work and come back later  

**What makes it stand out**  
All of this happens **after** the video is processed and previewable — creators edit with confidence because they’ve already seen the processed result.

---

### 6. Thumbnail picker and custom thumbnails

**What it is**  
The platform auto-generates 6 thumbnail options. Creators pick one, or upload their own custom image.

**Why it matters**  
- Thumbnails drive clicks — they are the “cover” of the video  
- Used on browse pages, search results, and **social media link previews**  
- Custom thumbnails allow branding and clarity (e.g. text overlays)  

**What makes it stand out**  
Balance of **automation** (zero effort) and **control** (custom upload) — good for both casual creators and professional publishers.

---

### 7. Subtitles (captions)

**What it is**  
Creators can upload subtitle files for different languages so viewers can read along while watching.

**Why it matters**  
- Accessibility for deaf and hard-of-hearing audiences  
- Better understanding in noisy environments  
- Broader reach for non-native speakers  

**What makes it stand out**  
Shows commitment to **inclusive publishing**, not just video for hearing-only audiences.

---

### 8. Visibility controls

**What it is**  
Three visibility modes:

| Mode | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Public** | Listed on browse, search, channels, and tags |
| **Unlisted** | Only people with the link can watch |
| **Private** | Only the creator and admins can access |

**Why it matters**  
- Creators can test, share internally, or publish broadly  
- Supports workflows like “internal review link” vs “public launch”  

**What makes it stand out**  
Visibility is **independent of approval status** — flexible governance without forcing everything to be all-or-nothing public.

---

### 9. Scheduled publishing

**What it is**  
Creators can set a future date and time for a video to go live. After admin approval, the platform publishes automatically at that time.

**Why it matters**  
- Coordinate launches (e.g. Monday 9 AM)  
- No need for someone to manually click “publish” at midnight  
- Supports marketing and editorial calendars  

**What makes it stand out**  
Combines **creator planning** with **admin approval** — suitable for newsrooms, campaigns, and course releases.

---

### 10. Submit for approval and moderation workflow

**What it is**  
When a video is **Ready** (processed successfully), the creator clicks **Submit for Approval**. Admins see it in a **Moderation Queue**, where they can:

- **Approve** — content accepted, not yet public  
- **Publish** — make it live  
- **Reject** — send back with a reason; creator can fix and resubmit  

**Why it matters**  
| Problem | How Streamora helps |
|---------|---------------------|
| Inappropriate content | Reviewed before public |
| Low quality / wrong format | Caught before brand damage |
| Legal or policy risk | Human gate before exposure |

**What makes it stand out**  
**Dual gate:** both the **creator** and each **video** can be controlled. New creators stay invisible until trusted. This is the defining difference from open upload platforms.

---

### 11. Admin dashboard and moderation queue

**What it is**  
A dedicated area for platform operators:

- See videos waiting for approval  
- Filter by status (pending, approved, published, rejected, takedown, archived)  
- Publish approved videos  
- Take down or archive published videos if needed  
- Monitor failed processing jobs  

**Why it matters**  
- Central control for editorial and compliance teams  
- Clear audit trail of what was approved and when  
- Operational visibility when something breaks  

**What makes it stand out**  
Built for **operators**, not bolted on. Moderation is a first-class product surface, not a hidden database.

---

### 12. Public discovery — browse, search, channels, and tags

**What it is**  
Viewers can:

- Browse all public videos  
- Search by keywords  
- Explore **channel** pages (e.g. “Sports”, “Education”)  
- Explore **tag** pages (e.g. “tutorial”, “news”)  

**Why it matters**  
- Videos aren’t useful if nobody finds them  
- Categories match how audiences think (“show me sports content”)  
- Search supports intent (“find the onboarding video”)  

**What makes it stand out**  
Locale-aware names and descriptions — discovery works in the audience’s language, not only English.

---

### 13. Share pages and social media previews

**What it is**  
Every published video has a **share page** with:

- Embedded video player  
- Title, description, and thumbnail  
- One-click share to **WhatsApp, Facebook, X (Twitter), LinkedIn**  
- Copy link, title, or full caption  
- Short links for easier sharing  
- **Rich previews** when pasted in chat apps (title + image + description)  

**Why it matters**  
- Most video growth happens through **sharing**, not browse  
- Professional previews increase click-through on WhatsApp and social feeds  
- Creators and marketing teams get share-ready URLs  

**What makes it stand out**  
Share pages are designed for **how people actually distribute content** in messaging apps — especially important in mobile-first markets.

---

### 14. Smooth video playback (adaptive streaming)

**What it is**  
Videos play through adaptive streaming: the player automatically switches between standard and HD quality based on the viewer’s internet speed.

**Why it matters**  
- Fewer buffering spinners  
- Works on phones, tablets, and desktops  
- Viewers stay watching instead of leaving  

**What makes it stand out**  
Same class of technology used by major streaming services — delivered automatically for every uploaded video.

---

### 15. Creator and video analytics

**What it is**  
Creators see dashboards with:

- Views and unique viewers  
- Play starts and completions  
- Completion rate (how many watched to the end)  
- Traffic sources (direct link, share, search, channel, tag)  
- Daily trends and top-performing videos  

**Why it matters**  
- Creators learn what works  
- Organizations measure ROI on video programs  
- Data informs what to produce next  

**What makes it stand out**  
Privacy-respecting measurement (hashed viewer identity, no raw personal tracking in analytics events) while still giving creators actionable insight.

---

### 16. Content governance (takedown, archive, restore)

**What it is**  
Admins can remove published videos (takedown) or archive them, with reasons recorded. Restored videos can go live again if appropriate.

**Why it matters**  
- Respond to copyright, policy, or safety issues  
- Document why content was removed  
- Support compliance and partner trust  

**What makes it stand out**  
**Governance lifecycle** beyond “publish once forever” — important for enterprise and media partners.

---

### 17. Reliable cloud infrastructure

**What it is (in plain terms)**  
Streamora runs on Google Cloud:

- Secure file storage for originals and processed videos  
- Background job processing so uploads never block the website  
- Database for users, videos, and approvals  
- Ready for global CDN delivery in production  

**Why it matters**  
- Scales as audience grows  
- Industry-standard security and durability  
- No single laptop or server holds all the videos  

**What makes it stand out**  
**Self-managed on GCP** — you own the data and the roadmap, unlike being locked into a third-party video SaaS with limited control.

---

## End-to-end story (one paragraph you can tell)

> A creator uploads a video from their phone. Streamora stores it safely, then automatically creates thumbnails and streaming versions. The creator adds title and description in their language, picks a thumbnail, and submits for approval. An admin reviews the video, approves it, and publishes it. Viewers find it through browse, search, or channels; watch it smoothly on any connection; and share it on WhatsApp with a rich preview. The creator sees how many people watched and where they came from.
